About SmartBuy

SmartBuy is a fully dynamic multi-platform price comparison system. No hardcoded products — search anything.

Universal Search

Search any product — grocery, electronics, fashion, furniture, pet food, baby care, and everything in between.

Live Price Comparison

Real-time Playwright scrapers fetch current prices from Amazon, Flipkart, and Meesho simultaneously.

RapidFuzz Matching

Adaptive similarity scoring filters irrelevant results. Works for electronics, groceries, and everything else.

Dynamic Filters

Filters are generated from actual search results — no predefined dropdowns. Brand, price, rating, discount, and more.
